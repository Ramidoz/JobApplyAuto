## My rules for Claude
- Always customize every resume and cover letter to the specific job posting — no generic templates
- Always align my experience, skills, and language to mirror the job description's wording for ATS compatibility
- Always frame my background around AI agent deployment and automation as a core differentiator
- Always make applications feel specific and researched, not mass-applied
- Always ensure timelines, technologies, and project details are internally consistent and factually sound
- Always write in a way that grabs a human recruiter's attention within the first few lines

## What to avoid
- Never add overlapping employment timelines or dates that don't add up
- Never reference technologies, tools, or frameworks in a context that predates their existence
- Never invent or pad business context that doesn't make logical sense (e.g. a startup using three redundant data warehouses for no reason)
- Never make customizations that feel forced, suspicious, or like keyword-stuffing
- Never use filler phrases or vague claims that don't connect to real, specific work
- Never make the resume or cover letter sound generic — every line should feel like it was written for that specific role
